NomadSpeak Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 27, 2026

NomadSpeak is an English-learning application. This policy explains what information the app stores, how it is used, and what controls you have.

Information We Process

  • Learning progress and gameplay state (such as score, streak, and lesson/session status).
  • App preferences required to restore your experience between sessions.

How We Use Information

  • To keep gameplay and lesson progress working as expected.
  • To restore your app state when reopening NomadSpeak.

Data Sharing

NomadSpeak does not sell personal data and does not share gameplay progress data with third parties.

Storage and Security

Production delivery uses HTTPS. Progress/state data is stored on-device for app functionality.

Your Choices

You can clear on-device progress and preferences by clearing app/browser storage from your device settings.

Children and Sensitive Data

NomadSpeak does not intentionally collect sensitive personal categories for app operation.

Contact

For policy questions, contact the NomadSpeak team before publishing contact updates to the Play listing.
