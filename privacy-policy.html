NomadSpeak Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 27, 2026
App ID: com.nomadspeak.mobile

This Privacy Policy explains how NomadSpeak ("we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use the NomadSpeak mobile application and related services (the "Service").

1) Information We Collect

Based on the current app implementation, NomadSpeak stores limited data needed for language-learning gameplay, including progress and in-app preferences.

Data category Examples Purpose Shared or sold
App activity / gameplay progress Lesson progress, streak, score, unlock state Core app functionality and progress continuity No
App preferences Local experience preferences and session flags Maintain and restore app behavior between sessions No

2) How We Use Information

  • Provide core language-learning gameplay and session continuity.
  • Save and restore your local progress between sessions.
  • Maintain app stability and expected user experience.

3) Data Sharing and Selling

We do not sell personal information. We do not share learning progress data with third parties for advertising.

4) Data Storage, Retention, and Security

  • Gameplay and preferences are stored on-device for functionality.
  • When network transfer is used for app delivery or content, HTTPS is required in production.
  • Data is retained until removed by the user (for example by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app).

5) Your Controls and Rights

  • You can reset local progress by clearing app storage in Android settings.
  • You can stop future data collection by uninstalling the app.
  • If region-specific law applies (for example GDPR/CCPA-style access or deletion rights), contact us at the support address below.

6) Children

NomadSpeak is an educational app and is not designed to knowingly collect sensitive personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and delete it if required.

7) Third-Party Services and SDKs

Current baseline: no advertising SDK and no third-party analytics/crash-reporting SDK declared in production policy scope. If this changes, this policy and Play Data Safety disclosures must be updated before release.

8) International Transfers

The current baseline keeps gameplay progress data on-device. If server-backed accounts are introduced later, this section will be updated with transfer and hosting details.

9) Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy over time. We will revise the effective date above when changes are published.
